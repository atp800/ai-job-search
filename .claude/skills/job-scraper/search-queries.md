# Remote job search queries

Search primarily for entry- and mid-level roles. Include unlevelled titles when their duties and requirements fit. Search titles individually, including common UK and North American variants such as “executive”, “associate” and “specialist”.

- **Default search.** Start with priorities 1–3 and the Australian sponsorship search. Also run a remote ML query from priority 4 and a remote marketing query from priority 5. Start with AI model evaluation and programmatic campaign roles, then rotate through the other titles on later searches.
- **Broad search.** Cover all six priorities, with general Python and data roles last. Search several titles from each group and deduplicate postings across groups.
- **Focused search.** For a request such as “remote machine learning”, “remote marketing” or “Python”, concentrate on the corresponding list across sectors. Include healthcare employers and employers in other industries.

## Search locations and sources

Target employers are saved alongside these settings in [companies.json](companies.json). Check every saved employer on default, broad and focused searches in addition to the boards below. To manage the list, say “Add a company to my job search” or “Show my target companies”.

Search United Kingdom, Canada and worldwide remote separately. Include US and other international employers that hire remotely in a relevant country. Search by employer location only as a discovery aid. Use the remote filter where available.

Use LinkedIn across all categories and FreeHire for technical roles. Supplement the portals with direct employer postings from universities, research institutes, contract research organisations, pharma employers, scientific publishers and medical communications agencies. Use the site queries below as starting points, substituting individual titles and rotating UK, Canada, worldwide and international location terms.

## Location and eligibility filters

- Prefer fully remote roles whose published work-location policy covers both the UK and Canada. Quote the role-specific wording or link to the employer's policy. Record any need for employer agreement to transfer after the move.
- Keep UK-only remote roles as UK options and Canada-only remote roles as potential post-move options. Mark each with the location constraint. UK work permission is confirmed. Canadian visa approval is targeted for February 2027. Mark Canada-only roles as conditional on approval and a compatible start date. The target month is not a confirmed start or move date.
- An international headquarters, a remote tag, flexible hours or sponsorship language alone does not establish permission to work across borders. Mark missing or ambiguous terms for verification. Exclude clearly incompatible work locations. For the remote search, exclude regular office attendance and field-based roles. Use the Australian sponsorship search for the in-person exception. Flag occasional travel.
- For each shortlisted role show permitted work location, viability after the move, and any outstanding authorisation question. Hours and time zones have no preference penalty.
- Include openings posted within 14 days or with a future deadline. Mark unknown posting dates and exclude closed roles.
